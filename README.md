###  ADF Fashion Sales Performance Dashboard
A real-world business analytics project developed from sales and customer-order records from a fashion business.
The project involved transforming manually maintained business records into a structured dataset, cleaning and organizing the data, analyzing sales performance over time, and developing an interactive dashboard.

**Key analysis included:** Sales performance over time,Highest and lowest sales periods,Revenue trends,Product/category performance,Customer and order analysis,Business performance KPIs

**Key outcome:**
The dashboard provides a visual overview of sales performance and helps identify periods of strong and weak sales activity that can support business planning and decision-making.

### Employee Performance & Workforce Analytics Dashboard
An HR analytics project designed to transform employee records into actionable workforce insights.
The project focuses on analyzing employee demographics, salary information, performance, tenure, leave and attrition-related indicators.
**Tools:** Excel, Power Query, Power BI, DAX.

**Key analysis included:** 
 Employee headcount,Gender distribution,Salary analysis,Department performance,Employee tenure,Performance trends,Attrition indicators,Data-quality issues

**Key outcome:**
An interactive Power BI dashboard that allows management to explore workforce patterns and identify areas requiring further investigation.

### Business Operations & Process Improvement Analysis
A business-process analytics project focused on understanding how operational activities move from customer order through processing, production and delivery.The project examines how business processes generate data and uses that data to identify potential bottlenecks, delays and areas for operational improvement.

**Key analysis included:**
Order processing performance,Operational turnaround time,Delayed orders,Process bottlenecks,Performance KPIs,Workflow analysis,AS-IS and TO-BE process mapping

**Key outcome:**
A combination of process mapping and data analysis designed to help businesses understand where operational inefficiencies occur and how workflows can be improved.
