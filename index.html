About Me

I'm a Business & Data Analyst with hands-on experience in business operations, data organization, analysis, reporting, and dashboard development. My practical experience includes transforming business bookkeeping and customer-order records into structured Excel data and using analysis to understand sales performance and support business decision-making.

I use Excel, SQL, Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to clean, transform, analyze, model, and visualize data. I am particularly interested in the intersection of business processes and data—understanding how an organization works, identifying patterns and inefficiencies, and turning data into actionable business insights.

My goal is to help organizations make better decisions by connecting data, processes, business problems, and actionable insights.
